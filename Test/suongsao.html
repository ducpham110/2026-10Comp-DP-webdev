The “Grass Jelly with Coconut Milk Dessert", also known as “Chè Sương Sáo Nước Cốt Dừa”, is a nostalgic childhood favourite that you will find on almost every bustling street corner in Vietnam. This treat works its magic by refreshing, earthy herbal jelly with rich, sweet coconut milk and a splash of simple syrup. Many people love the bouncy texture filled with creamy flavours and served chilled over crushed ice. If you are looking for the ultimate way to beat the heat, let's dive into making this cool comfort food together!

Ingredients:
Grassjellly part:
Rock sugar (30 mL)
White sugar (100 g)
Grassjelly powder (60 g)
Water (1 L)
Coconut milk part:
Coconut milk (500 mL)
Unsweetened milk (200 mL)
White sugar (70 g)
Tapioca starch (10 g)
Cooking salt (3 g)

Instructions:
Step1: Combine 60g of grass jelly powder and 100g of granulated sugar in a bowl and mix well. This is the secret to ensuring the grass jelly powder dissolves better in water and doesn't clump. Next, add the mixed grass jelly powder to a pot and then add 1 liter of water. Stir thoroughly until the grass jelly powder dissolves completely, then let it soak for about 10 minutes.

Step2: In a pot, stir constantly at low heat for a few minutes. When the grass jelly starts to become clear, glossy, thickened, and hard/heavy to stir, then turn off the heat. Quickly! pour the grass jelly into molds or bowls, let it COOL! (do not put in the fridge when it hot), then place it in the fridge for 1-2 hours to set.

Step3: After 1-2 hours in the fridge, take it out and cut into small cubes.

Step4: Now! We will doing the coconut milk to eat with glassjelly. Add 200ml of water to a pot, bring to a gentle boil, then add 70g of white sugar and cook until dissolved. Dissolve 10g of tapioca starch in a little water, then gradually add the dissolved starch to the sugar syrup, stirring constantly to prevent lumps. Cook until the tapioca starch is translucent, then add 500ml of coconut milk, 200ml of unsweetened milk, and 3g of salt, stirring well. Turn off the heat when the mixture just starts to boil again.


Step5: Add 1 tablespoon of basil seeds to water and soak for about 15 minutes until they have fully expanded. You can also substitute them with chia seeds; these are also delicious and highly nutritious.


Step6: For an extra touch, you can prepare additional accompaniments—such as pomegranate seeds (water chestnut bits), agar jelly, nata de coco, tapioca pearls, or fresh fruit—to enhance the flavor and nutritional value of the dish.
To serve, fill half the glass with shaved ice, then add the grass jelly and 30ml of rock sugar syrup (adjust the sweetness to your liking). Next, add the basil/chia seeds and any other toppings you have prepared. Finally, pour the coconut milk over the top, and you have a refreshing, satisfying dessert that doubles as a thirst-quenching treat.
